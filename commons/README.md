# Commons

The Commons is the public knowledge layer of Anitelos.

Its lifecycle separates raw ideas from discussion, proposals, adopted knowledge and historical material. A Drop is not automatically a claim of truth or an instruction to implement anything.

## Current layout

- `drops/` — raw, attributed or anonymous contributions and historical research material
- `questions/` — questions that remain open or have been resolved
- `discussions/` — active technical and philosophical discussion
- `proposals/` — formal proposals and RFCs
- `knowledge/` — currently adopted knowledge and specifications
- `archive/` — superseded public material and safe historical records
- `indexes/` — cross-references between topics, claims and decisions

Publication state and knowledge state are deliberately separate. See the repository `DROP-SAFETY.md` before publishing raw material.
