## Promotion Rule

No idea moves into current architecture merely because it appears repeatedly or
feels intuitively human. Promotion requires a precise claim, alternatives,
ethical/privacy analysis, an experiment, retained results and documented
limitations. Implementation itself remains open to challenge and supersession.
