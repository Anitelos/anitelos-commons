# The Painted Porch

> **Status:** `[SPEC]` named Commons deliberation space. A dedicated platform is not implemented.

**The Painted Porch** is the gathering place of the Anitelos Commons—where people meet around Drops, discuss ideas, challenge propositions, examine evidence and shape what may move forward.

The name recalls the painted public porch of the ancient Athenian agora: an open boundary where people gathered and philosophy developed. Here it means a welcoming human space for evolving thought, not a governing elite or a claim that discussion produces truth.

## What belongs here

- records or summaries of discussions that need durable provenance;
- objections, counterclaims and missing-evidence requests;
- meeting notes and decisions to branch, pause or formalise an idea;
- links connecting Drops, evidence, proposals and earlier decisions;
- affected-user experiences, including non-technical reports and minority configurations.

A discussion may remain unresolved. It may produce several competing proposals or return to a Drop. Moving through the Porch does not make a claim accepted or true.

## Current participation

Until a dedicated Commons interface exists, the Painted Porch may be expressed through GitHub issues, pull-request review, recorded meetings and Markdown discussion records in this directory. Public records must follow [DROP-SAFETY.md](../DROP-SAFETY.md); private or unsafe source material must not be committed merely for provenance.
