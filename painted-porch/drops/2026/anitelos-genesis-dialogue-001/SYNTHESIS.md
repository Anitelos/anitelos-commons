## 1. Governance & Reputation Evolution

* **Original Thought:** Anonymous execution usage should directly influence contributor governance voting power.
* **Challenge:** Execution volume can be Sybil-manipulated; volume does not equal technical expertise; flat reputation scores create dangerous social credit metrics.
* **Evolution:** Contributor reputation scores were removed. Standing attaches to **components, claims, and environmental outcomes** (`Contributor ──► Component ──► Outcome`), not humans. Usage provides diagnostic evidence, not raw linear voting power.
* **Status:** `[RESEARCH]`
* **Reconsider-If:** A privacy-preserving, zero-knowledge, Sybil-resistant proof-of-usage protocol is empirically demonstrated.

## 2. Licensing & Non-Enclosure Evolution

* **Original Thought:** Create a custom "Anitelos Reciprocal Non-Enclosure License" alongside Apache 2.0.
* **Challenge:** Custom licenses create legal fragmentation, ambiguity, and developer adoption friction.
* **Evolution:** Core engine and kernels utilize the established **GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)**. Schemas and protocol definitions use **Apache-2.0** for interoperability; creative modules declare their own licence. The Covenant defines ethical non-enclosure commitments.
* **Status:** `[INVARIANT]`

## 3. Associative Drops & The Ingredient Principle

* **Original Thought:** Informal analogies (like indirect lighting or oil in water) are too weak to include in formal knowledge bases.
* **Challenge:** Discarding informal observations loses the historical associative bridges that led to breakthroughs.
* **Evolution:** Created the **Drop Ingress Protocol**. Low epistemic weight does not mean zero connective value. Drops serve as intellectual ingredients preserving the unbroken associative path (`DROP ──► NOTE ──► EXPERIMENT ──► PAPER`).
* **Status:** `[SPEC]`

## 4. Founder Fade-Out & Platform Independence

* **Original Thought:** The originator directly steers the early project while governance mechanisms and a community do not yet exist.
* **Challenge:** Early practical control could fossilise into permanent authority or a centralized single point of failure.
* **Evolution:** **Founding provenance grants historical attribution, not perpetual authority.** All objects are migration-ready with stable hashes (`sha256`), making GitHub a 2026 replaceable organ.
* **Status:** `[INVARIANT]`
