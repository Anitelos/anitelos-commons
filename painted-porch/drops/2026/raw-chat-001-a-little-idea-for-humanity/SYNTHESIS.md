## 1. Two Related but Distinct Projects

### Individual AI continuity

The neural harness, KV/context experiments, vault, salience, prediction and
model customisation explore how one user might retain continuity while models,
runtimes and helper processes change.

**Current status:** `[SPEC + HYPOTHESIS + RESEARCH]`

### Human knowledge and people infrastructure

The Cosmic Wiki and Node Library are primarily for humans: preserving claims,
questions, evidence, disagreements, provenance and useful connections across
domains. Their purpose is not merely to feed a companion more context.

**Current status:** `[SPEC + RESEARCH]`

Keeping these purposes separate prevents the human Commons from becoming a
private memory database and prevents the personal harness from becoming a
centralised social profile.

## 2. Relevance Without Advertising Surveillance

The proposed knowledge layer aims to help a person encounter material and
people relevant to their actual questions, projects and learning path without
selling that relevance to advertisers.

The desired connector is epistemic rather than promotional:

- surface a useful weak idea to an expert who would otherwise never encounter it;
- connect disagreement by the exact contested claim rather than political tribe;
- translate jargon and explain why material may matter to the reader;
- let private context remain local while users explicitly choose what becomes public;
- preserve minority environments and unpopular branches rather than optimise only for volume.

This remains a design goal, not evidence that algorithmic matching will
automatically create understanding or social harmony.

## 3. “Super Sea” and Blackwall Analogy

The Cyberpunk Blackwall reference is a fictional analogy for the risk of an
unbounded, poorly governed information/agent environment. The proposed answer
is not one universal controlling wall. It is layered boundaries:

- local private cognition;
- explicit publication and capability boundaries;
- inspectable public knowledge objects;
- provenance, moderation, forks and plural governance;
- transport and hosting that remain replaceable.

The analogy is illustrative, not a technical threat model.

## 4. Linux and Modular/MAX/Mojo

Linux communities and the Modular/MAX/Mojo ecosystem are discussed as possible
technical and cultural participants if Anitelos becomes useful. They have not
endorsed Anitelos and are not dependencies or promised partners.

Potential alignment includes local execution, inspectability, hardware breadth,
custom kernels and open development. Potential friction includes licensing,
runtime maturity, proprietary boundaries, governance expectations and the cost
of maintaining specialist integrations.

**Current status:** `[OPEN POSSIBILITY]`

## 5. Licence and Governance Tension

Strong reciprocity may discourage some adopters while permissive licensing may
permit enclosure. Proof-of-usage may represent affected users while creating
privacy, Sybil and power-concentration risks.

These tensions remain research questions. Neither popularity nor the
originator's preference settles them permanently.

## 6. Success Is Not Virality

A durable, useful Commons with a real Linux and edge-hardware population would
already be meaningful. Growth should be evaluated through sustained utility,
knowledge quality, diversity of environments, user sovereignty and survivable
governance—not follower counts or mythology around the originator.

## 7. Current Central Proposition

> Personal AI continuity and shared human knowledge can support each other
> without becoming the same database, the same authority or the same privacy
> boundary.

This is a proposition to test, not a conclusion declared true.
