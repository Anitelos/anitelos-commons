# LICENSE

## Status: PENDING LEGAL REVIEW

This repository will be licensed under an established open-source license selected after consultation with qualified open-source IP counsel.

**Current placeholder:** The intended licensing framework combines:
- An established copyleft software license (candidate: AGPL v3, GPL v3, or EUPL 1.2)
- A separate non-enclosure covenant documenting the Commons principles
- Potentially different licenses for code vs. documentation (e.g., CC-BY-SA 4.0 for docs)

### Why We're Not Publishing a Custom License Yet

See `LEGAL-DESIGN-NOTES.md` for the full reasoning. In summary:

1. **Custom licenses are legally risky.** Once thousands of contributors rely on ambiguous language, fixing it becomes nearly impossible.
2. **Established licenses are battle-tested.** AGPL, GPL, and others have decades of court precedent and community trust.
3. **The real work is in the covenant, not the license.** The philosophical and governance principles belong in COMMONS-COVENANT.md, not LICENSE.md.

### What We're Protecting

- ✅ Reciprocal transparency for core engine improvements
- ✅ Commercial freedom for applications built on Anitelos
- ✅ Attribution integrity and provenance preservation
- ✅ No unilateral enclosure of Commons material
- ✅ Sovereign reversion rights for local nodes

### What Happens Next

1. **Legal Review:** Consult qualified open-source IP counsel to recommend:
   - Which established license(s) best encode our principles
   - Governance structures for enforcement (e.g., non-profit steward)
   - Contributor agreement strategy (if needed)
   - Trademark registration for "Anitelos"

2. **Finalize Licenses:** Update LICENSE, LICENSES/, and COMMONS-COVENANT.md with chosen framework.

3. **Announce Publicly:** Go public with a clear, defensible legal foundation.

### Until Then

- The code in this repository is released under a **provisional commons understanding**: treat it as open, shareable, and reciprocal, with the understanding that formal licensing will be retroactively clarified once counsel advises.
- All contributions are accepted under the assumption they will be protected by an open-source license with non-enclosure guarantees.
- No one should rely on this repository for production use until final licensing is in place.
