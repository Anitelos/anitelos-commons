# Cosmology at the Edge of END — Proof, Model, Metaphor & Myth

> **Status:** `[DROP + RESEARCH]` Questions and pushbacks for interdisciplinary review.  
> **Boundary:** This document does not present END Theory as cosmology or use physics as proof of its philosophical claims.

## Why keep this Drop?

Cosmic images helped END Theory ask questions about beginnings, domain fade, singularity, continuity and telogenesis. The same images can also make a philosophical proposition sound scientifically established when it is not.

This page preserves both the curiosity and the correction. Its purpose is to invite physicists, cosmologists, philosophers of science, information theorists and careful general readers to identify category errors, missing scholarship, alternative explanations and testable forms of the questions.

## Evidence vocabulary

| State | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Observation** | A measured phenomenon, with instruments and uncertainty |
| **Model** | A mathematical account that explains observations and makes predictions |
| **Inference** | Something concluded indirectly from observable effects |
| **Hypothesis** | A proposed explanation that must expose possible tests or falsifiers |
| **Metaphor** | An image that helps thought travel between domains without establishing equivalence |
| **Myth** | A meaning-bearing narrative; dangerous only when presented as measurement or proof |

“Myth” is not being used as a synonym for stupidity or falsehood. Myths can carry human meaning. They become epistemically misleading when their narrative force is substituted for evidence.

## Questions, present boundaries and END uses

| Question | Current scientific boundary | END may ask | Pushback END must retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Does the universe have an end?** | The observable universe has a horizon. That is not evidence of a physical wall or nothing beyond it. The whole universe may be finite or infinite. | What knowledge lies beyond any node's observable domain? | An observation limit is not an ontological edge. |
| **Was the Big Bang a pivot point?** | Standard cosmology describes expansion everywhere from an early hot, dense state—not matter exploding from one location into empty space. | Can a genesis node become a local origin of expanding relationships? | A Blender origin, philosophical centre and cosmological expansion are different concepts. |
| **What existed before the hot early universe?** | Unknown. Some models make “before” meaningful; others do not. | **Was dark matter there before the Big Bang?** | Dark matter is inferred in the early universe, but its identity is unknown. No observation establishes a pre-Big-Bang dark-matter state. | Could unseen structure seed later visible domains? | Dark matter attracts and structures matter; it is not the established driver of accelerated expansion. |
| **Are cosmic voids pockets of nothing?** | Voids are underdense, not known to be absolute nothing. They may contain sparse matter, dark matter, radiation and fields. | What happens when a domain has few observable or traversable edges? | Low density is not non-existence; an unobserved edge is not proof that no edge exists. |
| **Is a black-hole singularity the centre of a sphere?** | A classical gravitational singularity is not simply a transform pivot. It marks geodesic incompleteness or breakdown of the classical description; rotating solutions differ from non-rotating ones. | Does apparent finality sometimes reveal the limit of a map? | END cannot infer what physically continues beyond a theory's breakdown. |
| **Does a black hole invert, bounce or create another universe?** | Bounce and black-hole/white-hole models exist, but they are unverified. Information cannot presently return as a report from beyond an event horizon. | Could terminal collapse in one description become genesis under another? | A compelling symmetry is not an observation. No multiverse follows from END. |
| **Does the cosmos repeat infinitely?** | Cyclic and bounce cosmologies are research families. Current accelerated expansion does not establish a future recollapse or endless repetition. | Is telogenesis an open-ended pattern of phases producing conditions for later phases? | “Open-ended” is not literal infinity, recurrence or identical repetition. |
| **Are we still the Big Bang?** | The matter-energy and physical history from which bodies formed belong to cosmic evolution. | **Is knowledge fundamental to existence?** | Physics uses formal notions of information in specific theories; it does not establish END's broader metaphysical definition of knowledge. | Is knowledge the most consequential transferable continuity of relationships? | Relation, information, knowledge, truth and consciousness must not be collapsed into one word. |

## The strongest scientifically safe formulations

### The horizon

> The edge of observation is not necessarily the edge of existence.

NASA explains that the Big Bang was not a centred firecracker and that the universe has no known centre in the ordinary sense. [NASA Webb Big Bang Q&A](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/big-bang-q-and-a/)

### The early universe

> We are not debris sitting outside the Big Bang. We are among the present transformations of the early universe's matter-energy and structure.

The cosmic microwave background is observable remnant radiation from the early universe, not a photograph of an external explosion. [ESA on the cosmic microwave background](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Cosmic_Microwave_Background_CMB_radiation)

### Dark matter and dark energy

> Dark matter is inferred through gravitational edges; its underlying node remains unidentified.

Dark matter helps structure galaxies, while dark energy names whatever is associated with accelerated expansion. They are not interchangeable. [NASA dark matter](https://science.nasa.gov/dark-matter/) · [NASA dark energy](https://science.nasa.gov/dark-energy/)

### Singularities

> At a singularity, the map does not deliver a final physical answer. The classical map becomes incomplete.

General relativity predicts singular behaviour under stated conditions, while the need for quantum gravity leaves the physical interior unresolved. [Einstein Online on the singularity theorem](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spotlight/the-singularity-theorem/)

Quantum-bounce models demonstrate that continuation can be formulated mathematically under particular assumptions; they do not establish that real black holes produce new universes. [Example Physical Review Letters model](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.101501)

### Stellar collapse is not combustion

A stellar-mass black hole can form when the core of a sufficiently massive star can no longer maintain pressure against gravitational collapse. Nuclear burning and the core's composition help determine how that loss of support occurs, but black-hole formation is not chemical combustion and matter is not known to be simply “crushed out of existence.” Some collapses may produce visible supernovae; others may collapse more directly.

At ordinary densities, electromagnetic interactions and quantum-mechanical pressure help stabilise matter. In a neutron star, neutron degeneracy pressure and nuclear interactions can resist further collapse. Above the relevant mass limit, no known stable stellar configuration prevents continued collapse. Classical general relativity then predicts singular behaviour, but that does not provide a tested quantum description of the final interior state.

> **The scientifically responsible ending is not “an infinitely small point explains everything.” It is that the classical description becomes incomplete where quantum gravity is expected to matter.**

Black holes therefore offer END a boundary image, not physical confirmation: an apparent final state in one description may reveal where the map stops answering. [NASA on black-hole types](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/types/) · [Einstein Online on changing space and time inside a black hole](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spotlight/changing_places/)

## What would count as progress?

This Drop should not become `CURRENT` merely because its imagery feels coherent. Progress would include:

- corrections from relevant specialists with cited sources;
- precise definitions that survive counterexamples;
- separation of philosophical, computational and physical claims;
- mathematical formulations where the claim is intended to be mathematical;
- predictions or tests where the claim is intended to describe nature;
- documented rejection of claims that cannot survive evidence;
- and revised metaphors that remain useful after the physics is corrected.

## Closing boundary

END Theory is allowed to look toward cosmology for questions. It is not allowed to borrow cosmology's authority without doing cosmology's work.

> **The myth may open the question. The model must expose its assumptions. Observation decides what nature permits us to claim.**
