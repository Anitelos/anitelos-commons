# The Edge-Node Universe — Modular Production Map

> **Status:** `[SPEC]` Production order and continuity guide. Scene completion
> is tracked separately in `experience/scenes.json`.

## Production principle

The film is built as replaceable cinematic nodes rather than one irreversible
render. Each scene must work independently, connect to a declared theory
section and remain capable of revision or supersession.

## Visual grammar

- **World:** recognisable human spaces opening into immense relational fields.
- **Nodes:** sparse points with meaningful variation, never decorative particle noise.
- **Edges:** thin and imperfect; appear only when a relationship is being expressed.
- **Current knowledge:** cool white or restrained iridescent teal.
- **Unresolved/provisional knowledge:** warm amber.
- **Lost or unavailable paths:** absence, dim silhouettes and broken continuity rather than evil black fog.
- **Camera:** slow observational movement; scale revealed gradually.
- **Tone:** quiet awe, intellectual honesty, melancholy becoming cautious hope.
- **Avoid:** neon cyberpunk, a giant central AI brain, magical certainty, utopian victory and villain caricatures.

## Audio grammar

- Narration is measured and observational rather than promotional.
- Silence is allowed to carry loss and uncertainty.
- Music begins with restrained texture, not a trailer impact.
- Interface sounds remain physical and local before the world expands.
- Every spoken clip receives a transcript and WebVTT captions.

## Cold Open — production specification

### Image sequence

1. An ordinary room before dawn; no futuristic machinery.
2. A long AI-assisted conversation is implied on a local screen.
3. The screen abruptly goes black.
4. The camera waits rather than cutting immediately.
5. Faint relational nodes emerge from the dead display.
6. Some fragments dissolve; a few connect far beyond the room.
7. The image opens into the night sky as the central question arrives.

### Narration

> A conversation ends. The screen goes dark.
>
> Yet what disappeared was never only a sequence of words.
>
> It was a path: question to discovery, objection to revision, one fragile
> connection opening the way to another.
>
> When the carrier vanishes, who carries the journey forward?

### Evidence boundary

The sequence illustrates the continuity problem. It does not claim that every
conversation deserves permanent retention, that machine systems possess human
memory, or that visualised edges are literal structures inside cognition.
